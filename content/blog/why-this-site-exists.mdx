I'll keep this one short, because it's the first thing here and you don't owe me your afternoon.

I spent a long career around money and the people trying to hold onto it. Somewhere along the way I noticed that when ordinary people went looking for honest information about gold, they mostly found two things: spreadsheets they couldn't read, or ads designed to scare them into a phone call. Neither one respects the reader.

**So this is the third option.** Plain answers to the questions people actually ask before they buy gold — what it costs, where to get it, how the price works, and whether it even belongs in a retirement account. No countdown timers. No "the dollar collapses next Tuesday." Just the way I'd explain it to my brother-in-law if he asked me at a barbecue.

A few promises about what you'll read here:

- **I'll give you the number and the tradeoff,** not a command to buy.
- **When something's a rule, I'll say so. When it's a judgment call, I'll say that too.**
- **I don't sell metal, and I'm not your advisor.** Everything here is information, not advice — verify the current figures and talk to a professional before you move real money.

If you're brand new to this, the best place to start is the guide on [how to buy gold](/guides/how-to-buy-gold). It's the one I'd hand to anyone asking where to begin.

Glad you're here.

— Gary
