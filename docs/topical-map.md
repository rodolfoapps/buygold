# Buy Gold Insider — Topical Map (v0)

This is the planning artifact that turns the site into a real semantic content
network, using the Koray Tugberk framework (see the `semantic-seo` repo). It is
intentionally a living document — the foundation ships with the **root** and the
first cornerstones; the rest is the runway.

## Central entity & source context

- **Central entity:** gold (and, by extension, precious metals).
- **Source context:** a no-hype, educational authority that helps a careful,
  retirement-minded reader understand and buy gold. We inform; we do not sell
  metal and we are not advisors.
- **Central search intent:** *understand and buy gold sensibly* — the central
  entity ("gold") + source context ("buy / own / protect savings") augmented
  with predicates like *how, what, cost, safe, worth it*.
- **Audience:** roughly 55–65, skeptical of Wall Street, worried about
  retirement, allergic to doomsday-ad pressure.

## Site shape

- **Core section** (deep, close to home, where authority concentrates):
  buying gold, gold prices, gold for retirement. This is where the cornerstones
  live.
- **Outer / author section** (flatter coverage that broadens authority):
  silver and other metals, history of gold, gold vs. other assets.

## Publishing notes (from the framework)

- Lead with the **declarative answer**, bold it, then qualify.
- Use **numeric values** and ranges, not vague adjectives.
- Every cornerstone links to/from the **root** (`how-to-buy-gold`).
- Match **anchor text** to the target article's title; max ~3 uses of the same
  anchor across a page.
- Keep the **knowledge base consistent** — no contradictions across articles
  (e.g., premium ranges and the "no home storage for IRA gold" rule must agree
  everywhere).
- Refresh cornerstones on a patternless cadence (`updated` field) to signal
  momentum.
